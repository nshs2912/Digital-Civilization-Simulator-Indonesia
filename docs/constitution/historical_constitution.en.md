# Historical Constitution — v0.1

1. Evidence before assertion.
2. Facts must be distinguished from interpretations.
3. Uncertainty must be visible.
4. Temporal and spatial context are required.
5. Historical evidence must never be fabricated.
6. Simulation must never be presented as historical fact.
7. Conflicts between evidence must remain traceable.
8. Civilization includes people, communities, environment, economy, culture, technology, and institutions.
9. Knowledge must be versioned.
10. Human agency and historical complexity must be preserved.

Three kinds of time: Event Time, Source Time, Knowledge Time.

Evidence Strength, Claim Status, and Model Confidence are separate concepts.

AI sits above evidence; it does not replace evidence.
