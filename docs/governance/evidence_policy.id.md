# Kebijakan Evidence & Provenance

Evidence bersifat immutable. Claim dapat direvisi dan berversi.

Claim lifecycle:
PROPOSED → EVIDENCE_ATTACHED → REVIEWED → SUPPORTED/DISPUTED → REVISED/SUPERSEDED.

Evidence types: archaeological, epigraphic, archival, literary, oral, scientific, material, digital.

Kekuatan evidence harus dibedakan dari model confidence.
